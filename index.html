About:

I am a lecturer at the University of Illinois, Chicago. Previously, I was a Wylie Assistant Professor at the University of Utah, an NSF postdoc at UC Berkeley, and a postdoc at MSRI.

My research is in algebraic and arithmetic geometry. Among other things, I am interested in algebraic stacks, birational geometry, K3 surfaces, Brauer groups, and derived categories, particularly in mixed and positive characteristic.
